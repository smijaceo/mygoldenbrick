# Landing page: argument and copy deck

Direction: taste-skill `product-performs` (dealt in round 2). The hero follows the
approved canvas board "1. I make page, literally".

**Idea sentence:** On this page, *the name "I make page"* becomes *a hero that makes the
visitor's page as they type*.
**Live thing:** the builder. Type a name and an area, and a real agent site, with real
listings, fills in beside the form.

## Argument (claim → proof)
1. **You get a real site in a minute** → the live builder in the hero.
2. **It runs your day, not just your site** → how-it-works slider: type, publish, buyer
   asks, it's at the top of Today.
3. **The parts agents actually use are all here** → six feature cells, each with a real
   fragment.
4. **Straight answers to the obvious worries** → FAQ about the MLS feed, domains, email
   and phones.
5. **Start now** → a closing sentence with the visitor's name as the blank.

Pricing is dropped: there are no real prices yet (plan constraint: no invented proof).

## Copy

**Hero**
- H1: Type your name. Get your site.
- Sentence: Your website, home search and lead inbox build themselves while you watch.
- Fields: Your name / Where you sell.
- Button: Make my page.
- Link: Sign in.

**How it works** (heading: "From your name to your first lead")
1. Type your name — the site is built around you.
2. Pick where you sell — listings for your area fill in.
3. Publish your page — buyers can search, save and ask.
4. A buyer asks — they're at the top of your Today list.

**Features** (heading: "Everything in one login")
- **A site that's yours.** Your colours and words. Change a line and see it live.
- **Home search with a map.** Filters buyers actually use, on any phone.
- **One inbox for every enquiry.** Contact forms, tour requests and listing questions, newest first.
- **A pipeline you can drag.** From New to Closed, with notes and tasks on each person.
- **Today, before coffee.** Who's waiting on you, what's due and who looked at your site.
- **Replies in one place.** Answer from the lead's page; the history keeps itself.

**FAQ** (heading: "Fair questions")
- *Does it show real MLS listings?* — Not yet. You see sample listings now; your MLS feed
  plugs in once you have an IDX agreement with your board.
- *Can I use my own domain?* — Yes, that's coming. For now your site lives at
  imakepage.com/s/your-name.
- *Does it send email and texts?* — Replies are saved to each lead today; real delivery is
  switched on when you connect an email provider.
- *Will it work on my phone?* — Every screen is built for one thumb: check leads and reply
  between showings.

**CTA:** "Make a page for [your name]" (the blank is the input; "Go" is the button).
Below it: "You'll pick a password next. Nothing to pay to try it."

**Footer**
- Statement tile: "Websites and lead follow-up for real estate agents."
- Nav: Sign in · Make my page · Demo site (rendered as a list, no dots)
- City tile: "Made in Omaha, Nebraska"
- Email tile: hello@imakepage.com (placeholder: confirm the real address before launch)
- Wordmark tile, plus an accent tile "Make my page" linking to sign-up.
